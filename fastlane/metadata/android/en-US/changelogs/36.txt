1.3.0. Widgets and apps can draw in 3D now: try sunset heist, a city to drive around, and canyon run, a flight down a canyon. More new example apps: boomtown, a city builder, sky forge, a shooter with upgrades, and last outpost, tower defense. The store is split into widgets and apps, and faces are now full-screen widgets with a tab of their own. Games that ask for 60 frames a second keep them, and today, what's new, ntfy and github inbox refresh when you pull their list down.
